The whole point

Somewhere better
than your desk.

Be honest: reading on the same screen you work on all day never really happens. Ten more open tabs is a chore, not a treat.

A printed issue is the same reading in a different headspace. Take it to the park, a coffee shop, or a train window. Paper doesn't ping, and nobody ever lost an afternoon to a magazine's notifications.

Long reads work better this way, too. A 6,000-word essay is a slog to scroll and a pleasure to hold.

Black and white interior, color cover, typeset like the magazine it is.

The fine print

Ten trees per issue

Yes, we print on real paper (recycled or FSC-certified stock). And for every issue we print, we plant ten trees, which is roughly a hundred times the paper we use. Want to check our math? The running tally is printed in every copy.
